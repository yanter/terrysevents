Summers were hot, sticky, and full of open windows and box fans.&#x20;

You learned where the breeze came in and which chair was coolest by evening.&#x20;

Cold drinks, shade trees, and a hose in the yard did the job.&#x20;

We didn’t complain much because that was just life.&#x20;

You adapted, slowed down, and kept going.&#x20;

Somehow, we slept just fine and woke up ready for another day.&#x20;

It taught resilience and appreciation for simple comfort.
